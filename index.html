Our Drinks

We offer a great range of local beers, as well as draught lagers and ciders from across the globe.

We’re passionate about wine, and our list offers something for everyone. With a selection of champagnes and sparkling wines whether you are celebrating or not, and white, red and rosé wines specially selected from the most popular regions, we’re confident you will find something you love.

Our Food

We take great pride in providing freshly-prepared, country-style cooking with influences from all around the world. We introduce a new menu each season, and with all of our food made to order, our chefs can tailor each dish exactly to your liking.

If you have any dietary requirements, suffer from any allergies or intolerances please make us aware of your needs either when booking or on arrival. Please ask if you are not sure and we will do our best to answer your query. Our food and drinks are prepared in food areas where cross contamination may occur and our menu descriptions do not include all ingredients.
